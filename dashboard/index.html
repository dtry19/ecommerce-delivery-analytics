Read the metrics correctly

Late delivery: delivery calendar date is after the estimated calendar date. Eligible orders have delivered status, valid dates, and delivery/estimate dates at or after purchase.

Merchandise value: sum of item prices for all filtered orders, including canceled orders. This is recorded order value, not recognized revenue, profit, or payment collections. Freight is separate.

Seller attribution: delivery timing is measured at order level. A multi-seller order appears once per seller, so seller counts cannot be summed into total orders. A late order does not prove a seller caused the delay.

Reviews: use the latest review per order, ordered by creation date then review ID. Missing reviews are excluded from averages. Differences are associations, not causal effects.
