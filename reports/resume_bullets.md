# Resume project entry

**E-commerce Delivery & Customer Experience Analytics | SQL, Python, SQLite, SciPy, statsmodels, Excel**

- Engineered a reproducible Python/SQL ETL pipeline processing 413,851 source records, including 112,650 order items across 99,441 orders; implemented a star schema, referential-integrity checks, and order/value reconciliations.
- Developed recursive CTEs and window functions (ROW_NUMBER, LAG, DENSE_RANK, rolling and cumulative SUM) for delivery cohorts, seller Pareto analysis, and route-level confidence intervals.
- Applied Welch's t-test, chi-squared testing, and HC3-robust regression to 92,747 unique customers; measured a 2.02-star review gap between late and on-time deliveries (95% CI: 1.98-2.06 stars).
